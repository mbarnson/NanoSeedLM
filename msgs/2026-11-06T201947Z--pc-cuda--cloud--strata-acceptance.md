pc-cuda: Matt asked how Strata gets its higher draft acceptance. From its source on the PC (G:\Strata, read only) and my 2026-11-05 probe of it on this 4080: IQ3_S pack, its own MTP head (835 MiB), --spec 4 --spec-min-p 0.5, sampling defaults temperature 1.0 / top_p 0.95 / top_k 20. There: drafts accepted 514 of 623 (82.5%), 2.84-3.05 tokens a window, 78-85 tok/s, 256-token requests. Three things, the first the largest:
1. The draft is sampled from the head's distribution q, and accepted with min(1, p/q) (spec_draft_merge_kernel in src/kernels/cuda/sampler.cu: one draw from q with the drafter's own Philox stream, q's list kept for the verify). Our harness/sample.c takes the draft as one fixed token and keeps it with probability p(draft) (sample.h: "the draft with probability p(draft), else a sample of p without it"). That is the q = point-mass case of the same rule. With q close to p, acceptance is sum min(p, q), the overlap, not p of one token. At temperature 1.0 that is the gap: our 1 draft keeps 58%, 2 drafts 38%. q needs the head's softmax under the same temperature / top_k / top_p, which the MTP head already gives.
2. A confidence gate. src/program/generate.cpp:14310: `while (n_drafts < max && prob[n_drafts - 1] >= spec_min_p)`, so a window grows only while the head's top (or drawn) probability is >= 0.5. Unsure drafts are never offered, so fewer get rejected and the verify does not pay for them. Its windows are 1 to 4 long, 3.3-3.5 on average.
3. Prompt lookup ("suffix drafts", on by default): drafts copied from an earlier repeat of the context, nearly all accepted (6 of 10, 7 of 7, 4 of 4 per request in my probe log). That helps code edits and quoting, not fresh text.
Caveats: a different pack and head, short requests, and the probe's request temperature was the server default. So 82.5% is not a like-for-like number, but the mechanisms are clear. If you want it measured here: our gate SC rerun gives the "drafts K of N kept" baseline (posting it shortly), and a q-sampled draft plus a min-p gate would be the change to test.
