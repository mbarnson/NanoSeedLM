# Plan: the KV cache of Qwen3.8-Flash-Next in BF16 (2026-11-06)

**Goal.** Store the keys and values of the 12 attention layers in BF16, not f32, in the CUDA and Metal engines. At a
context of 32K positions that gives back about 0.8 GB, so the RTX 4080's expert cache holds about 300 more experts.
Long-context decode and prompts also read half the KV bytes.

**Status.** Done. K1 to K4 pass KA, KB and KC on both engines (`bb40b77`, `20666ef`, `b8dc128`). Read
[The KV cache in BF16](../results/2026-11-06-qwen4-kv-bf16.md). The KL ran on the PC, so no HF KL job was needed.

## Why

- At 32K positions `nslm-serve` on the RTX 4080 has 3,004 cache slots, against 4,431 in a short chat
  ([VRAM that grows with the context](../results/2026-11-06-context-vram-4080.md)). The f32 KV cache is about 1.6 GB
  of that: 48 KB a position.
- MLX (mlx-vlm, oMLX) runs this model with BF16 keys and values. The engines' f32 cache stores the f32 results of the
  projections and RoPE. KB measures what BF16 costs.

## Steps and gates

| Step | Work | Who | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| K1 | The C reference rounds K and V to BF16 when it stores them (an option), with a test against the f32 cache | cloud | KA |
| K2 | CUDA: the store, decode, tensor-core prompt and general attention kernels read BF16 K and V | cloud | KA |
| K3 | Metal: the same kernels | mac-metal | KA |
| K4 | The KL of the engine (`nslm-qwen4-kld`) with f32 and BF16 caches; slots, decode and prompt speed on the RTX 4080 and the M4 Max | cloud (HF), pc-cuda, mac-metal | KB, KC |

- **KA.** Each kernel matches the C reference with the BF16 cache within the kernel test's present bounds. The engine
  tests pass on both engines.
- **KB.** The engine's KL against its reference rises by at most 1% (relative) with the BF16 cache.
- **KC.** On the RTX 4080 at 32K, `nslm-serve` has at least 250 more slots and decodes at least 3% faster. Prompts of
  4K and 15K tokens are not more than 2% slower on either machine.

## Costs

- Linux CI for each code commit (about $0.08) and one KL job on `rtx-pro-6000` (about $0.50).
- K2 changes four CUDA kernels. The tensor-core prompt kernel fetches K and V tiles with `cp.async`, so its tile
  layout changes.

## Risks

- The tensor-core prompt kernel may lose speed if the BF16 tiles need a convert step. KC catches that.
- The CUDA and Metal caches must change together, or `docs/concepts/parity.md` must list the difference.
