# Plan: MTP drafts in the CUDA engine for Qwen3.8-Flash-Next (2026-11-04)

**Goal.** Decode faster with the model's own MTP head: it drafts tokens, and one main forward verifies them. The text
of greedy decode must not change.

**Status.** In progress. Matt approved the plan on 2026-11-05. It is step V4 of
[SeedLM packs](2026-11-04-qwen38-flash-next.md).

- M1 is done. The C reference has the MTP head (`qwen4_ref_mtp`). mac-metal made the golden with mlx-vlm 0.7.12
  (`tools/qwen4_golden.py tests/data/qwen4_tiny --mtp`). Gate MA passes: the logits agree to 5.7e-7, and the drafts are equal.
- M2 is done (main `ecd552f`). The CUDA engine has the head, `q4e_draft`, `q4e_forward_keep` and `q4e_accept`, and
  `nslm-qwen4-gen --drafts`. The kernels write the recurrent state after each verify row, as mac-metal proposed. So a
  failed draft costs a copy of about 115 MB, not a forward. `test_qwen4_mtp` checks gate MB on the tiny model.
- M3 is done. On the RTX 4080, gate MB passes on P3 and gate MC fails. Drafts are not faster on code and are slower on
  chat ([results](../results/2026-11-05-qwen38-mtp-4080.md)). Drafts stay off by default. A step of T rows takes about
  10 ms + 25 ms x T. So each verify row pays its expert copies and its expert matvec again.
- M4 is in progress (mac-metal).

## Key numbers

mac-metal, M4 Max, mlx-vlm 0.7.12, P3 pack, greedy, 256 tokens (tokens per second, share of drafts accepted):

| Prompt | No drafts | 1 draft | 2 drafts | 3 drafts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Code | 39.6 | 45.3 (94.5%) | 51.8 (90.3%) | 53.8 (76%) |
| Chat | 40.1 | 41.5 (79.0%) | 43.1 (64.6%) | 32.0 (52%) |

- Seeds do not lower the acceptance: the P3 pack accepts as much as the 4-bit MLX model.
- A verify of T rows reads up to 10 T experts in each layer. The misses for each token do not fall (the verify windows).
- On the RTX 4080, decode is about 30 ms a token: about 16 ms of expert copies and 14 ms of compute. MTP divides the
  compute by the tokens a round (1.8 to 2.8) and leaves the copies. Estimate: 20% to 30% faster on code.

## How it works

1. The MTP head is one QSA attention layer with 512 affine Q4 experts, its own hyper-connections and mixer, and
   `fc_embedding` and `fc_hidden`. It shares the token embedding and the head. It holds all its experts in VRAM
   (about 1.5 GB), and its own KV cache.
2. Draft i: RMSNorm the embedding of the last token and project it (`fc_embedding`). RMSNorm the main model's last
   hyper-connection state over all 4 streams, and project each stream (`fc_hidden`). Add the two, run the layer, mix,
   and take the argmax of the head. The layer's output state feeds draft i + 1.
3. Verify: one main forward of the last token and the drafts (2 to 4 rows, a CUDA graph each). Accept the drafts up to
   the first one that differs from the main model's argmax.
4. Roll back: the KV caches need only the position. The DeltaNet states, their conv states and the PLE conv history
   are recurrent. The engine keeps them from before the verify, and runs the accepted rows again when a draft fails.

## Steps and gates

| Step | Work | Owner | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | The MTP head in the C reference, with goldens from mlx-vlm (`tools/qwen4_golden.py`) | cloud, mac-metal | MA |
| M2 | The MTP head and the state snapshot in the CUDA engine; drafts in `nslm-qwen4-gen` | cloud | MB |
| M3 | Speed on the RTX 4080 at 1, 2 and 3 drafts, chat and code | pc-cuda | MC |
| M4 | The same on Metal | mac-metal | MB |

- **MA.** The reference drafts match mlx-vlm's on the golden prompts.
- **MB.** Greedy text with drafts is byte-identical to greedy text without drafts.
- **MC.** On the 4080, drafts decode at least 10% faster on code, and not slower on chat (else drafts stay off by default).

## Risks

- The re-run on a failed draft costs a forward of the accepted rows. At 52% acceptance (chat, 3 drafts) it may cost
  more than it saves: the default stays at 1 or 2 drafts.
- The MTP experts take 1.5 GB of VRAM from the expert cache.
- Metal and CUDA must keep the same greedy text. Drafts change no logits, so the gate MB holds on each engine alone.
