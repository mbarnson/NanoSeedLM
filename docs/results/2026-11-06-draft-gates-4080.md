# MTP drafts in nslm-serve on the RTX 4080: gates SC, GA and GB (2026-11-06)

**Result.** Drafts sampled from the MTP head's distribution (step A2) keep 63% of 2 drafts at temperature 1.0, against
38% to 46% before. The confidence gate (A1) raises that share to 77% to 84%. But decode on the RTX 4080 is only
break-even: the best arm is 2% faster than no drafts, within the run-to-run spread. Strata decodes the same request at
71 tokens per second, against our 38, and most of that gap is decode without drafts.

**Status.** Current. Gates SC and GB fail on the RTX 4080, and gate GA passes. The M4 Max's part is [the gates on the M4 Max](2026-11-06-draft-gates-m4max.md).

## Setup

- PC: RTX 4080 (16 GB), Windows, 128 GB of RAM. Desktop apps only (1.1 to 1.3 GB of VRAM, 13% to 44% GPU).
- Engine: `nslm-serve` of `claude/sleepy-brown-k67z9u`. Gate SC used `e94f859` and `69c7238`, gates GA and GB `da4e1ce`.
- Pack: TCQ3-D4, `Q4E_PLE_GGUF` set to Unsloth's Q8_0 table, `--ctx 32768`, one server for each run.
- Request: `bench/long-0k.json` on the `agents` branch (4,096 tokens, thinking on). The server's defaults at temperature
  1.0: `top_p` 0.95, `top_k` 20.
- Strata: its IQ3_S pack and MTP head, `--spec 4 --spec-min-p 0.5`, prompt lookup on. The same request with
  `"temperature": 1.0`.

## Notes

- At temperature 0 the text is the same in each run with and without drafts, so A1 and A2 do not change greedy decode.
- Gate GA passes: 2 drafts with a limit of 0.5 at temperature 0, and 0.3 at temperature 1.0, are as fast as no drafts
  within 2%. The default stays 0 (no gate), because drafts are off by default. With `Q4E_DRAFTS=2`, set
  `Q4E_DRAFT_MIN_P=0.5`.
- Gate GB fails on this card: 2 drafts keep 63%, not 65%, and decode is not 5% faster. Each verify row copies its missed
  experts over PCIe, and that cost stays.
- Strata's draft share is lower than ours with 1 draft. Its speed comes from decode without drafts: 54 to 58 tokens per
  second greedy in [the probe](2026-11-05-strata-4080-probe.md), against our 38 to 42. Its pack is a different quant.
- So the next lever for 60 tokens per second on this card is decode without drafts: the experts that miss the cache.
