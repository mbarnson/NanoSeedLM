# 3-bit seeds (S3) for the 64 GB class of Qwen3.8-Flash-Next (2026-11-04)

**Result.** S3 seed experts alone use 3.02 bits for each weight. Their mean KL divergence against BF16 is 0.142,
about twice UD-IQ3_XXS (0.0735 at 3.28 bits). With a third of the experts in P3 (3.35 bits), the KLD is 0.112 to 0.118 for
`down_proj`, random or chat-and-code thirds. With the hot third of the text's own first 14,883 tokens in P3, it is
0.0765, 4% above UD-IQ3_XXS for 2% more bytes.

**Status.** Current. One text. No engine decodes S3 yet: the packs are emulated.

## Setup

- S3 search: all 24,576 experts at C = 12, P = 4 (`nslm-moe --codec s3`, the CUDA search of `nslm/searchc.cu`), with
  Unsloth's imatrix and n0 = 64, all 65,535 seeds. HF Jobs `rtx-pro-6000x8`, job `6aebffa0c78863f9c633226b`: 52 min,
  12.9 GPU-hours, about $21.
- KLD: llama.cpp against BF16 with the BF16 PLE table, as in [the packs against BF16](2026-11-04-qwen38-kld-vs-bf16.md).
  HF Jobs `rtx-pro-6000x4`, job `6aec0d150e0338d1bb805b53`, 33 min, about $6.
- Mixes: `nslm-qwen4-emulate` with the S3 banks, the P3 and P4 packs, and the tier maps of
  [hybrid tiers](2026-11-04-qwen38-hybrid-tiers.md). `freqwt2` maps the most used experts of the first 14,883 tokens
  of the KLD text itself, as a session's own history would. Those tokens hold about 11% of the scored tokens.
- Bits: the format's bits. A block of 12 weights holds a 16-bit seed, four 4-bit coefficients and a 4-bit exponent.
  A row's last block is padded (2,568 of 2,560 and 648 of 640 columns). The banks store each exponent code in a byte,
  so they are larger than the format.

## Notes

- S3 is 68% above Unsloth's curve at 3 bits, and P3 is 19% above it at 4 bits. The seed layout loses more at 3 bits.
- At 3 bits, `down_proj` in P3 gains 4% over a random third. At 4 bits it gained nothing.
- A hot set from other text does not help. The text's own hot set gives 34% less KLD than a random third.
- A pack cannot know a session's hot set in advance. The expert cache learns it, so a tier that follows the cache can
  approach these rows. That needs both tiers of each expert, an engine that decodes S3, and a cache that loads the
  high tier of hot experts. None of these exist yet.
