# Seed packs and Unsloth packs of Qwen3.8-Flash-Next against BF16 in one harness (2026-11-04)

**Result.** We measured all packs in llama.cpp against BF16, with the BF16 PLE table. The P4 seed pack has a mean KL
divergence of 0.0408 at 4.50 bits for each expert weight. Unsloth's packs give 0.0509 at 3.97 bits and 0.0304 at 5.10 bits, so P4 is on
their curve. The P3 pack has 0.0600 at 4.00 bits, 18% more than UD-IQ4_XS at 3.97 bits. The order of the packs agrees
with Unsloth's table, so gate G5 passes.

**Status.** Current for the KL values. One text (wikitext-2), one reference run. The UD packs' expert sizes are too
small: our reader counted a new GGUF type (2.25 bits) as 0 bytes. The measured sizes are 0.03 to 0.05 bits larger,
for example UD-IQ3_XXS 3.331 bits and 50.29 GB. Read [the corrected curve](2026-11-05-trellis-mixes.md).

## Setup

- Machine: HF Jobs `rtx-pro-6000x4` (4 RTX PRO 6000, 1 TB of RAM), jobs `6aebf61d0e0338d1bb80569c` (Q8_0 and the UD
  packs) and `6aebfd40c78863f9c6332197` (Q8_0 again, P3, P4). Each job computed its own BF16 reference.
- llama.cpp `5ce06fc`, CUDA, all layers on the GPUs. `llama-perplexity` with `-c 2048 --chunks 64`: 64 windows of
  2,048 tokens, the second half of each window scored (65,536 tokens).
- Reference: Unsloth's BF16 GGUF ([unsloth/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-GGUF](https://huggingface.co/unsloth/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-GGUF)
  at `d962012`), with its BF16 PLE table. Every pack uses that same table file.
- Text: the wikitext-2 raw test split.
- Seed packs: emulated by `nslm-qwen4-emulate`. It writes the decoded seed experts of the P3 or P4 pack into the
  expert tensors of Unsloth's Q8_0 GGUF, rounded to Q8_0. The other weights are Unsloth's Q8_0 weights.
- Expert bits: from the GGUF headers (`blk.*.ffn_*_exps`), or the seed streams for P3 and P4.

```bash
hf jobs run --flavor rtx-pro-6000x4 -v hf://buckets/txgsync/nslm-qwen38:/b -e SRC=/b/src-7e6c9c4 -e OUT=/b/kld-t1 \
    nvidia/cuda:12.8.1-devel-ubuntu24.04 bash /b/src-7e6c9c4/tools/hf_llama_kld.sh
```

The second job set `SRC=/b/src-e8426e0` and `PACKS="Q8_0 P3 P4 ..."`.

## Notes

- Unsloth's packs give a curve of KLD against expert bits. On a log scale between UD-IQ4_XS and UD-Q4_K_XL, it gives
  0.040 at 4.5 bits (P4: 0.0408) and 0.050 at 4.0 bits (P3: 0.0600).
- Unsloth mixes its types by layer and projection. UD-IQ3_XXS uses IQ2_S (2.5 bits) for gate and up in layers 0 to 13
  and IQ4_NL for most `down_proj` tensors. It uses Q2_0 for `down_proj` in layers 8, 10 and 13.
- The emulation favours and harms the seeds a little. The seed packs keep Q8_0 dense weights (5.5 GB), and the UD packs
  use 4.5 GB. The Q8_0 rounding of the decoded seeds adds error.
- This harness gives each pack about half of Unsloth's KLD, as the text differs. The order is the same.
- The plan's expert budgets used pack sizes. These are the expert bytes from the headers.
