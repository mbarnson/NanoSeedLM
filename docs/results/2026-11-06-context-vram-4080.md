# VRAM that grows with the context on the RTX 4080 (2026-11-06)

**Result.** At a context of 32,768 positions, the CUDA engine keeps about 4.8 GB more VRAM than in a short chat. So
the expert cache of the RTX 4080 has 2,530 slots, not 4,431. Layer-major passes of at most 8,192 rows (not 32,768)
give back 474 slots. Decode is then 8% faster, and prompts of 3,916 and 14,718 tokens are 43% and 47% faster, with the
same text. The engine now takes passes of 8,192 rows under 32 GB of VRAM.

**Status.** Current.

## Setup

- PC: RTX 4080 (16 GB), Windows, the clang-cl build of `1a55b64` (the `Q4E_PASS_ROWS` option). The automatic expert
  cache and the default VRAM reserve. Desktop apps only, no other GPU work.
- Pack: the release TQ3-D4 (`txgsync/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-NanoSeedLM-TQ3-D4` at `7e5edb2b`).
- Runs: `nslm-qwen4-gen --ctx 32768`. Decode: the code prompt, 256 tokens, two runs. Prompts: `--raw --prompt-file`,
  3,916 and 14,718 tokens, 4 generated tokens.

## Results

What grows with the context:

| Buffer | Size | At 32,768 positions |
|---|---|---|
| The KV cache (f32, 12 attention layers, 2 KV heads of 256) | 48 KB a position | about 1.6 GB |
| The hidden streams of a layer-major pass | 40 KB a row | 1.3 GB (32,768 rows), 0.34 GB (8,192 rows) |
| The buffers of a prompt forward | about 0.25 MB a row | 1 GB (4,096 rows) |

The grid:

| Setting | Slots | Decode (tokens per second) | 3,916-token prompt | 14,718-token prompt |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| (a) the old defaults (passes of 32,768 rows, forwards of 4,096) | 2,530 | 34.85, 35.85 | 1,069 | 1,299 |
| (b) passes of 8,192 rows | 3,004 | 38.27, 38.22 | 1,530 | 1,911 |
| (c) (b), forwards of 2,048 rows | 3,210 | 39.48, 39.46 | 1,531 | 1,734 |
| (d) (b), forwards of 1,024 rows | 3,314 | 40.01, 40.05 | 1,344 | 1,471 |

The prompt columns are in tokens per second. Every decode run has the md5 `16994974`.

## Notes

- (b) is faster than (a) in every column. The prompts are faster too: the slots also hold the experts that a prompt
  pass copies. That is measured, not proven.
- Smaller forwards give more decode speed for slower long prompts. The default stays at 4,096 rows.
- `354e2e1` makes (b) the default under 32 GB of VRAM. GPUs of 32 GB or more keep passes of 32,768 rows: one pass on
  the RTX PRO 6000 ([one pass](2026-11-05-qwen38-one-pass-attention.md)).
- The KV cache in BF16 would give back about 0.8 GB more at 32K. That needs kernel work on both engines.
