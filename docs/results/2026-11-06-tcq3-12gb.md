# TCQ3 on a 12 GB GPU, emulated on the RTX 4080 (2026-11-06)

**Result.** With 4 GB more of VRAM kept free, the RTX 4080 acts as a 12 GB GPU. TCQ3 then loads with 1,087 expert cache
slots, against 3,324 with all 16 GB. It decodes 4,096 tokens at 27 tokens per second, and 2,048 tokens at 26 after a
prompt of 30,072 tokens. That prompt runs at 1,237 tokens per second.

**Status.** Current. A 64 GB PC is not emulated: this PC has 128 GB of RAM.

## Setup

- PC: RTX 4080 (16 GB), Windows, 128 GB of RAM. Desktop apps hold about 1,150 MiB of VRAM.
- Engine: the v0.1.0 Windows zip (`6becc62c`), `nslm-serve` with `run-TCQ3.bat`'s flags: Unsloth's Q8_0 PLE table and
  `--ctx 32768`.
- `NSLM_VRAM_RESERVE_MB=5120`: the default reserve of 1,024 MB on a card that drives a display, plus 4,096 MB.
- Pack: TCQ3 at `5f2a8599`. Requests: `bench/long-0k.json` twice, then `bench/depth-30k.json`, at temperature 0.

## Results

| Request | Decode (tokens per second) | Prompt (tokens per second) | Time to the first token |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4,096 tokens, short prompt | 27.2, 27.5 | | 1.10 s, 1.04 s |
| 2,048 tokens after 30,072 | 25.9 | 1,237 | 24.5 s |

With all 16 GB, TCQ3 decodes at 44 and 43 ([long runs on the RTX 4080](2026-11-06-long-bench-4080.md)).

## Notes

- The peak of VRAM in use was 11,389 MiB with the desktop's share, inside the emulated 12 GB (12,280 MiB).
- The decode is 38% slower than with 16 GB: the cache holds a third of the slots, so more experts come over PCIe.
- On a real 64 GB PC, the pack's 47.2 GB of experts and the page cache compete for RAM. The load may take longer, and
  the PLE rows may come from the disk more often.
- Raw data on the PC: `G:\devel\qwen4-runs\tcq3-12gb*`.
