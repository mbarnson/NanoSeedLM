# The KV cache of Qwen3.8-Flash-Next in BF16 (2026-11-06)

**Result.** Both engines now keep the keys and values of the attention layers in BF16, not f32. On the RTX 4080 at
32K positions the expert cache gets 379 more slots, and decode is 5.7% faster (40.8 against 38.6 tokens per second).
On the M4 Max a 15K-token prompt is 5% to 6% faster, and the memory at 16K positions falls by 0.4 GB. The KL of the BF16 cache against the f32 cache is at or below a noise
floor. That floor is the KL of a half BF16 step on the PLE inputs.

**Status.** Current. Plan K, steps K1 to K4 ([the plan](../plans/2026-11-06-qwen4-kv-bf16.md)).

## Setup

- Builds with the f32 cache: `1424085` (CUDA) and `20666ef` (Metal). Builds with the BF16 cache: `20666ef` (CUDA, K2)
  and `b8dc128` (Metal, K3). Each pair ran interleaved.
- PC: RTX 4080, clang-cl, the release TQ3-D4, `nslm-qwen4-gen --ctx 32768`, the automatic expert cache. Desktop apps
  only.
- Mac: M4 Max, TQ4L12 (4-bit dense weights), `nslm-qwen4-gen --raw --max-tokens 64 --ctx 16384`. The TQ4 download ran
  at the same time.
- Quality: `nslm-qwen4-kld --tokens 1024 --decode 256 --expert-cache-gb -1` with each build. The floor build is a local
  patch of pc-cuda's (not committed).

two packs.

## Notes

- The C reference rounds the keys (after RoPE) and the values to nearest even with `qwen4_ref_kv_bf16`. On the tiny
  model its logits move 1.1e-3 of 1.5, and no row's argmax changes. The kernels of both engines match it.
- The greedy text changes with BF16 rounding: TQ3-D4's 256-token decode on the PC, and two raw prompts that end
  mid-word on the Mac. The Mac's merge prompt gives the same text with both caches.
- The indexer's keys stay in f32.
