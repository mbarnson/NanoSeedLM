# Strata on the RTX 4080 PC: why its MTP drafts pay (2026-11-05)

**Result.** Strata decodes Qwen3.8-Flash-Next IQ3_S at 85 tokens per second with MTP drafts and 57 without, on the
PC where NanoSeedLM's P3 decodes at 41. A Strata step of T rows takes about 10.5 ms + 7.1 ms x T, and ours about
9 ms + 25 ms x T. Strata's rows cost less because they miss fewer experts and compute most misses on the CPU. Each of
our rows copies its missed experts over PCIe.

**Status.** Current. One PC, one prompt, two measured requests for each arm. Strata ran with Matt's configuration,
which includes lossy options.

## Setup

- Machine: pc-cuda's PC with Windows and an RTX 4080 (16 GB, PCIe 4). CPU: Ryzen 7 5800X3D (8 cores). RAM: 128 GB
  DDR4-3200. No other programs ran. The least available RAM was 64.7 GB.
- Strata as installed, with Matt's configuration `strata-iq3_s.json` unchanged:
  - Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-GSQ-RCO-IQ3_S experts.
  - `--spec 4 --spec-min-p 0.5`, `--vram-reserve-mib 1664`, and an automatic expert cache: 7.2 GB with MTP, 8.0 GB without.
  - PCIe fraction 0.55.
  - Two lossy options: a "speed projection" control vector on layers 4 to 44, and `STRATA_ROUTE_TAIL_SKIP` at its default (7).
- Each arm: `STRATA_DECODE_TIMING=1`. The arm without MTP drops `--mtp`. Code prompt, 256 tokens, temperature 0. One
  warm-up request, then two measured requests.
- Reference: Strata's [how it works](https://github.com/Niko1221/Strata/blob/main/docs/HOW_IT_WORKS.md) and
  [details](https://github.com/Niko1221/Strata/blob/main/docs/DETAILS.md).

## Notes

- Strata's GPU time hardly changes with the rows of a step (9.7 to 10.8 ms). The cost of each row is host CPU work on
  its missed experts. So a step of 3.45 rows gives 3 tokens for twice the time of one token.
- NanoSeedLM's step grows by about 25 ms for each row. The row pays the PCIe copies of its missed experts, and the expert
  matvec for each of its (row, expert) pairs.
- Without MTP, Strata misses about 2.2 experts a layer and token, and NanoSeedLM about 3.5. The causes may include:
  - smaller experts (3.44 bits against 4.0), so more experts fit in a GB
  - a smaller MTP footprint in VRAM
  - placement from a usage profile, with swaps during decode
  - the tail skip: a missed expert that every token of a step routes at rank 7 or lower is not computed.
- Strata's CPU computes an expert in about 85 µs on 8 cores (IQ3_S). The NanoSeedLM CPU seed kernel needs about
  235 µs for a P3 expert on the same CPU ([CPU seed rate](2026-11-05-qwen38-4080-cpu-and-streamed.md)).
- The windows reuse experts only a little: 2.0 misses a layer and row with MTP, against 2.2 without. Each CPU expert
  serves 1.31 rows.
