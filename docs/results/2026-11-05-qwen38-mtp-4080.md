# MTP drafts on the RTX 4080: the same text, not faster (2026-11-05)

**Result.** On an RTX 4080 with a 6 GB expert cache, P3 gives the same greedy text with 0 to 3 MTP drafts. Gate MB
passes. The drafts do not make decode faster. Code decodes at 29.7 tokens per second without drafts and 27.6 to 28.9
with drafts. Chat decodes at 29.2 without drafts and 22.7 to 28.7 with drafts. Gate MC fails, so drafts stay off by default.

**Status.** Replaced by [MTP drafts on the RTX 4080, again](2026-11-05-qwen38-mtp-4080-rerun.md). These runs waited on
PLE reads, on a PC near its RAM limit.

## Setup

- Machine: pc-cuda's PC. RTX 4080 (16 GB, PCIe 4), 128 GB RAM, Windows. No other programs ran. The runs pinned about
  116 GB of RAM. After the runs, the GPU was at 36 °C and 18.8 W. Power during the runs was not logged.
- Commit: `ecd552f` (main).
- Model: [Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-NanoSeedLM-P3](https://huggingface.co/txgsync/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-NanoSeedLM-P3) at
  `5753a67`, with its 54 `mtp.*` tensors.
- Two rounds. In each round, K = 0, 1, 2 and 3 ran in that order. `NSLM_VRAM_RESERVE_MB=2560`.

```bash
nslm-qwen4-gen --model P3 --prompt "Write a Python function that merges two sorted lists, with a docstring." --max-tokens 256 --expert-cache-gb 6 --drafts K
nslm-qwen4-gen --model P3 --prompt "Why do tide pools matter? Answer in three sentences." --max-tokens 256 --expert-cache-gb 6 --drafts K
```

## Notes

- A step of T rows is T - 1 drafts and their verify. On both prompts, it takes about 10 ms + 24 to 25 ms x T (a
  least-squares line through the four means). So the rows share only about 30% of a one-row step. Each row pays the rest again.
- Two costs may make up most of the 24 to 25 ms that each row pays:
  1. The PCIe copies of missed experts. The misses for each token do not change at verify windows of 1 to 4 tokens
     ([verify windows](2026-11-04-qwen38-verify-windows.md)). The plan estimates them at about 16 ms a token here.
  2. The decode expert matvec. It makes one pass for each (row, expert) pair. On the RTX PRO 6000 it took about 7 ms a
     token, and its bytes need less than 1 ms ([decode by format](2026-11-04-qwen38-decode-by-format.md)).
- Rows of one verify share experts. The tide-pools routing trace has 10 experts a token in each layer. A window of 2,
  3 or 4 tokens uses 8.01, 7.09 or 6.49 distinct experts a token. A matvec that reads each distinct expert once for all its rows
  may save up to a third of cost 2. It must keep the sums of each row in the order of a one-row forward, or gate MB
  fails. The copies stay, so the estimated gain for drafts on this PC is at most about 10%.
- The A/B keeps the cache at 6 GB in both arms. The head and 3 state copies take about 1.9 GB of VRAM. Without drafts,
  that VRAM can hold more expert cache, so this A/B favors the drafts.
- Round 1 without drafts on code (27.77) may include warm-up. In round 2 every draft count is slower than no drafts.
- The accepted shares are lower than in mac-metal's mlx-vlm study (plan table). The study's prompts may differ.
- Drafts may pay where few experts cross PCIe: experts in unified memory (Metal, plan step M4), or a VRAM cache that
  holds most experts. That is not measured on CUDA.
